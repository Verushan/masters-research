Over 100% does NOT mean over-adapting. It means behaviour moves more across
partners than the optimal response does, which is what being pushed around by
the partner looks like: a competent best response imposes a stable joint
policy, an incompetent agent gets dragged. Magnitude cannot tell those apart,
which is what the next measure is for.
